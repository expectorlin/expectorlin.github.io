📖 Biography

Bingqian Lin is currently a postdoctoral researcher at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, working with Prof. Cewu Lu. She received the Ph.D. degree in Electronics Information Engineering at Sun Yat-sen University, advised by Prof. Xiaodan Liang and Prof. Liang Lin at the Human Cyber Physical Intelligence Integration Lab (HCP-I2 Lab). She was selected for the 2025 China National Postdoctoral Program for Innovative Talents. She was a research intern at Huawei Noah’s Ark Laboratory focusing on vision-language pretraining from 2021 to 2023. As the first author, she has published multiple papers in top-tier international journals/conferences, including IEEE TPAMI, CVPR, Neurips, AAAI, etc. She also serves as a reviewer for prestigious international publications such as IEEE TPAMI, IEEE TMM, CVPR, NeurIPS, ICLR, etc. Her research interest lies in Embodied AI, Vision-Language Navigation, and Multi-modal Understanding.
